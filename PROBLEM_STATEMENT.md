# 1.3. ROADSOS

## 1.3.1. SHORT DESCRIPTION
The tool provides location-based access to nearby trauma centres, ambulance services, vehicle rescue services, police stations and emergency contacts during road accidents.

## 1.3.2. FULL DESCRIPTION
In the event of a road accident, victims and bystanders often face difficulty in quickly identifying and contacting the appropriate emergency services. Critical information about nearby trauma care centres, hospitals equipped to handle accident injuries, ambulance services, vehicle rescue providers, and police stations is often not easily accessible at the moment it is needed.

Delays in locating and reaching these services can significantly impact the "golden hour," the crucial period immediately after an accident when timely medical assistance can save lives and reduce the severity of injuries.

This tool aims to address this challenge by providing a location-based platform that helps users quickly identify and access nearby trauma centres, ambulance services, vehicle rescue facilities, police stations, and other emergency contacts. By integrating these services into a single, easy-to-use interface, the platform will enable faster coordination of emergency response, support bystanders in taking immediate action, and improve access to critical care and assistance for road crash victims.

## 1.3.3. KEY ASPECTS FOR CODERS TO INCLUDE
- Nearest Police Station, hospitals, ambulance services
- Towing services, nearest puncture shops, and showrooms
- Global applicability across countries
- Offline functionality and robustness in low-network conditions

## 1.3.4. EVALUATION CRITERIA
- Reliability and data accuracy
- Number of contacts fetched
- Offline functionality
- Innovation & additional features
- Information integration across countries
